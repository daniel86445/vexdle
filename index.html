        How to play

        1. You get one roll a day. Each roll fills 6 slots.
        2. Every slot draws from V5RC/VURC items with equal odds: parts, packs, kits, electronics, fields, game elements, game manuals and a few memes. An empty slot is 3× as likely as any single item.
        3. Every part earns XP from a simple formula: a category base (hardware 5, structure and motion 10, electronics 30, fields and kits 40…) plus bonuses for aluminum, high strength, value, pack size, the current season and vintage fields and manuals. Memes and Manual v0.1 cost XP. Hover any card, or check "How your parts scored," to see the math.
        4. Most XP comes from combos, which are matches based on item type, order, price, weight, size, material, season and more. There are combos, each worth a fixed amount of XP, and they all stack. The rarer a combo, the more it's worth. You only see a combo once you've hit it.
        5. Your total XP decides your award, from Didn't Pass Inspection up to World Champion. The day's #1 roll on the leaderboard gets Worlds Excellence.
        6. Avoid Override Game Manual v0.1.
